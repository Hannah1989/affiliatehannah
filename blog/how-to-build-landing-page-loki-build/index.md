## Step 2: Write Your AI Brief

This is the most important step. The better your brief, the better Loki's first draft.

You have two options:

**Option A — Write a prompt**

Describe your product in 2-4 sentences. Include:
- What it is
- Who it's for
- The main benefit or outcome
- The action you want visitors to take

Example prompt:
> "Landing page for an AI video tool called Fliki. It's for content creators and marketers who want to turn blog posts into videos without recording anything. Main benefit: save 5+ hours per video. CTA: start free trial."

**Option B — Paste a reference URL**

If you have a competitor's page or an existing site you like, paste the URL. Loki uses it as a style and structure reference while generating original copy for your product.

Hit **Generate** and wait about 10 seconds.

---

## Step 3: Review the First Draft

Loki returns a full page — hero section, features, social proof, pricing or CTA blocks, and footer.

Don't expect to love everything on the first pass. The goal is to get a strong foundation, then refine.

Look for:
- Is the headline compelling and clear?
- Does the structure flow logically?
- Are the sections relevant to your product?

Make a quick mental list of what needs changing. You'll fix it in the next step.

---

## Step 4: Refine with the AI Editor

Click any section and use natural language to make changes. This is where Loki's editor really stands out.

**Examples of what you can type:**
- "Make the headline more urgent"
- "Add a FAQ section after the features"
- "Change the color scheme to dark navy and white"
- "Make the CTA button bigger and more prominent"
- "Add a section showing 3 key benefits with icons"

Each instruction updates just that section — it doesn't regenerate the whole page. You stay in control.

For more precise edits, you can also:
- Click text to edit it directly
- Drag and resize blocks
- Change fonts, colors, and spacing manually
- Reorder sections by dragging

**Pro tip**: Start with the big structural changes (add/remove sections, change layout) before dialing in details like copy and colors. It's faster to work top-down.

## Step 6: Add Your SEO Details

Before publishing, fill in the SEO settings for your page:

1. Click **Page Settings** (usually a gear icon)
2. Set your **Page Title** — this appears in Google search results and browser tabs

Loki auto-generates these based on your content, but take 5 minutes to review and improve them. A better meta description means more clicks from Google.

## Step 8: Test Before You Promote

Before driving traffic to your page, run through this quick checklist:

- [ ] Open the page on your phone — does it look good on mobile?
- [ ] Click every button and link — do they work?
- [ ] Read the copy out loud — does it make sense?
- [ ] Is the CTA (call to action) clear and prominent?
- [ ] Is the FTC affiliate disclosure visible if you're promoting affiliate products?
- [ ] Does the page load quickly?

Fix anything that stands out, then you're ready to promote.

## Step 9: Drive Traffic to Your Page

Your page is live — now you need people to see it. Three fast ways to start:

**1. Add it to your bio link**
If you have a bio link page (like on Instagram or TikTok), add this landing page as a featured link.

**2. Share it in relevant communities**
Reddit, Facebook groups, Discord servers, LinkedIn — find communities where your target audience hangs out and share your page with genuine context.

**3. Create content around it**
A short reel showing the product in action, a carousel post explaining the problem it solves, or a tweet thread — all pointing back to the page.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Too many CTAs**: Pick one primary action per page. "Sign up free" or "Get the guide" — not both. Multiple calls to action split attention and hurt conversion.

**Weak headline**: Your headline should answer "what do I get and why does it matter to me?" in one line. If it doesn't, refine it.

**No social proof**: Even one testimonial, a number ("500+ users"), or a logo bar dramatically increases trust. Add it before publishing.

**Skipping mobile review**: 60-70% of visitors will view your page on mobile. Always check it before promoting.
