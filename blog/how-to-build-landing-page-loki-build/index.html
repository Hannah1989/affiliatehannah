What You'll Need

  • A Loki.Build account (free — no credit card required)
  • A clear idea of what your page is promoting
  • A domain name (optional — Loki provides a free URL to start)
Step 1

Sign Up and Start Your First Project

Go to loki.build and create your free account. Once you're in the dashboard, click New Project, give it a name, and choose Landing Page as the type.

Step 2

Write Your AI Brief

This is the most important step. The better your brief, the better Loki's first draft. You have two options:

Option A — Write a prompt. Describe your product in 2–4 sentences. Include what it is, who it's for, the main benefit, and the action you want visitors to take.

Example: "Landing page for an AI video tool called Fliki. It's for content creators who want to turn blog posts into videos without recording anything. Main benefit: save 5+ hours per video. CTA: start free trial."

Option B — Paste a reference URL. Paste a competitor's page or any page you like the style of. Loki uses it as a structure reference while generating original copy for your product.

Hit Generate and wait about 10 seconds.

Step 3

Review the First Draft

Loki returns a full page — hero section, features, social proof, pricing or CTA blocks, and footer. Don't expect to love everything on the first pass. The goal is a strong foundation to refine.

Look for: Is the headline compelling? Does the structure flow logically? Are the sections relevant to your product? Make a quick list of what needs changing.

Step 4

Refine with the AI Editor

Click any section and use natural language to make changes. Examples of what you can type:

  • "Make the headline more urgent"
  • "Add a FAQ section after the features"
  • "Change the color scheme to dark navy and white"
  • "Make the CTA button bigger and more prominent"
  • "Add a section showing 3 key benefits with icons"

Each instruction updates just that section — it doesn't regenerate the whole page. You stay in control. For more precise edits, click text directly, drag and resize blocks, or adjust fonts, colors, and spacing manually.

Pro tip: Start with big structural changes (add/remove sections) before dialing in copy and colors. Work top-down.
Step 5

Set Up Your Brand Kit

Go to Brand Settings and enter your brand colors, fonts, and logo. Every page you create from now on automatically uses these settings — huge time saver if you're building multiple pages.

Step 6

Add Your SEO Details

Click Page Settings and fill in your page title (appears in Google search results), meta description (150–160 characters), and URL slug (short and descriptive, e.g., fliki-free-trial). Loki auto-generates these but take 5 minutes to improve them — better descriptions mean more clicks from Google.

Step 7

Publish Your Page

Click Publish. Your page goes live instantly on a Loki-hosted URL with fast hosting, SSL (https://), mobile-responsive layout, and sitemaps — all included. If you're on the Pro plan, connect your custom domain under Domain Settings.

Step 8

Test Before You Promote

Run through this checklist before driving traffic:

  • Open the page on your phone — does it look good on mobile?
  • Click every button and link — do they work?
  • Read the copy out loud — does it make sense?
  • Is the CTA clear and prominent?
  • Is the FTC affiliate disclosure visible if promoting affiliate products?
Step 9

Drive Traffic to Your Page

Three fast ways to start:

  • Add to your bio link — paste the URL in your Instagram or TikTok bio link page
  • Share in communities — Reddit, Facebook groups, Discord, LinkedIn
  • Create content around it — a reel showing the product, a carousel, a tweet thread — all pointing back to the page

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too many CTAs — pick one primary action per page. Multiple CTAs split attention and hurt conversion.
  • Weak headline — it should answer "what do I get and why does it matter to me?" in one line.
  • No social proof — even one testimonial or a number ("500+ users") dramatically increases trust.
  • Skipping mobile review — 60–70% of visitors will view on mobile. Always check it.
